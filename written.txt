Q3. What happens to the bounding boxes with update + fullRefit?
the bounding boxes expand as their contained shapes move apart.
This causes the BVH to become invalid as this expansion may
catch new shapes inside a bounding box that are not supposed to
be there.

Q4. What happens with reset (rebuild every frame) instead?
bounding boxes no longer overlap with bounding boxes from
other nodes. This causes a correct grouping of shapes in
the BVH every frame. Frame rate has also significantly
improved.

Q5. Why are some circles drawn with their sides cut off?
this is because the BVH ray intersection test only checks
within the boundaries of the appropriate bounding box. If
a circle extends past the bounding box, collisions on the
protruding part will not be detected and will be drawn
cut off. The circles likely protrude due to off-by-1 errors
in creating the bounding boxes.
